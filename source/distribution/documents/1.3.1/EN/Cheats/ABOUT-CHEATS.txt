CHEATS AND BUILT-IN TOOLS - 1.3.1 PREPARATION

This package includes no Action Replay catalogue. Earlier Plus catalogues have
not been validated against this exact 1.3.1 candidate; changing the version or
filename does not establish compatibility. No legacy code has been relabelled.

IV consultants, EV training, the Shiny page and portable storage are game
features. They do not require an emulator cheat or plug-in. Use the
current-feature PDF in the main folder for their controls. The installed
summary-control component remains inactive and is not a player feature.

If a candidate-specific catalogue is prepared later, it needs its own identity
and effect checks. The maintainer's Italian device update preserved the existing
cheat activation state, but that does not validate the catalogue's effects or
the English target. Keep an untouched backup of your normal save. An old
catalogue or old savestate is not part of this package's file-reconstruction
verification.
