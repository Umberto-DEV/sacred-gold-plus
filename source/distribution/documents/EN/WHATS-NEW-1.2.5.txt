WHAT IS NEW IN 1.2.5

Mac validation: seven load/save/cold-restart cases with older saves, including non-default settings and the 252nd Bag slot. Keep a backup and start through Continue, not an older emulator quick state.

Includes all 1.2.4 sprite-copy and Waterfall-search fixes and earlier features. Game speed is unchanged. This is not a full new playthrough and does not establish the cause of the WaterMelonDS rendering report.
