**Pokémon Sacred Gold Plus** is an independent fan continuation of Sacred Gold Plus, built on Drayano's Sacred Gold for Nintendo DS. Explore Johto with the original Plus project's Fairy typing, additional Fairy moves and wider outdoor camera, alongside new settings and quality-of-life improvements.

Available in **English and Italian**.

**Windows and Mac:** choose the edition you want to play in, download its ZIP and extract it. Open **Sacred Gold Plus {{VERSION}}**, then **How-to-install-{{VERSION}}-EN.pdf** (English) or **Come-installare-{{VERSION}}-IT.pdf** (Italian). The installation PDF has numbered steps and real screenshots. **Whats-New-{{VERSION}}-EN.pdf** / **Novita-{{VERSION}}-IT.pdf** is beside it and introduces the current game features. `Manual` contains the original gameplay references.

**New to patching?** You can start directly from your own original HeartGold `.nds`; you do not need Sacred Gold or an older Plus version first. The guide shows how to check the ROM's SHA-256, choose **one** patch from `Patch/From Clean ROM/US` or `IT`, apply it with [Rom Patcher JS](https://www.marcrobledo.com/RomPatcher.js/), and verify the finished game. The ZIP selects the final game language; the US/IT folder selects the starting ROM language.

**Updating Plus?** Back up your game and save, then follow the guide's `From Plus` route for your existing version. Resume with **Continue** using the normal in-game save.

Each ZIP includes 25 recognized source routes, including original USA/Italian HeartGold and verified Plus inputs through V1.3. Match the full SHA-256 in the installation PDF and apply one patch. The target language follows the ZIP edition. Cheats are not required; no unverified legacy catalogue is included.

Use the two player ZIP links above. GitHub's automatic **Source code (zip / tar.gz)** files are for developers and are not needed to play. No ROM is included: use your own cartridge backup and do not download pirated ROMs.

## Game features

- **English and Italian editions:** translated dialogue, menus and interface elements, including an Italian naming keyboard with accented characters.
- **EV/IV tools inside the game:** inspect EVs with L and IVs with R in the Pokémon summary, and return to the normal stats with SELECT. No cheat is needed. The field Guide covers evolutions, items and TMs, events and training.
- **Adjustable difficulty:** optional trainer level increases and a separate setting for wild Pokémon level scaling, capped at level 100.
- **A dedicated Plus options page:** press SELECT in Options to manage difficulty, wild levels, Pokémon motion, NPC smoothing and Wi-Fi connection slot selection.
- **Classic HeartGold camera in 52 interiors and caves:** paths and objects are easier to see in the affected maps, while the wider Plus camera remains outdoors.
- **Optional Pokémon motion in battle:** both sides can move while waiting for an action and during menu selection. It uses the existing sprites and yields to the game's move, entrance and fainting animations.
- **Smoother town loading and quicker battle menus:** NPC loading is spread out when entering or leaving towns, and the Bag and party menus reuse data already loaded for the battle.
- **Expanded Bag and consecutive Rare Candy use:** 252 slots in the main pocket, expanded capacity in the other pockets, and a party menu that stays open after using a Rare Candy. Evolution and move-learning prompts follow the normal game flow.
- **Text and gameplay corrections:** over 200 English/Italian text fixes, Pokédex search repairs, corrected move names and dialogue, and fixes for item pickups, Bag handling and battle sprite copies.

- **IV consultants and EV training:** Silver and Gold Caps at the Violet and Blackthorn PokeMarts, quick EV services and free level/EV courses at the Route 34 Day Care. Choose real time or active playtime for courses; milestone rewards support existing adventures.
- **Classic and Pro shiny modes:** a second Plus page offers native shiny profiles, target chains and species search from the area's eligible encounter pool. Search respects the game's terrain, rods, time and progression.
- **Selected species and trainer improvements:** targeted stat and learnset changes across the roster, purposeful Plus trainer moves, 85-accuracy Will-O-Wisp and the Keen Eye evasion rule. These are specific changes rather than a complete later-generation ruleset.
- **Portable Pokemon storage:** press R in the ordinary field party menu to open the native PC interface in permitted contexts.
