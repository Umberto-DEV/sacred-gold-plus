THE MANUALS — ENGLISH


This folder holds documents by other authors, plus a game guide kept by
this project.


HOW FAR THEY CAN BE TRUSTED FOR 1.2

They are historical references, useful for encounters, items, evolutions
and events. They are not a claim that every entry has been retested on
1.2, and in several places they describe Sacred Gold before Plus changed
it: trainer teams and some move information may therefore differ.

In the trainer reference, the heading "v1.05" refers to an older Sacred
Gold guide, not to this continuation's 1.05.

The five PDF guides and the trainer reference are the original Sacred
Gold / Storm Silver documents, shipped exactly as their authors wrote
them. They are kept in English in both language downloads, with the
original credits intact: translating them would mean rewriting somebody
else's work and losing the exact match with the originals.
